# Teacher worksheet retakes

Students receive one attempt on each quiz or exam by default. A teacher can grant an individual student one additional attempt from the worksheet's attempt roster after the student submits.

1. Open the course worksheet and its attempt roster.
2. Find the student and select **Grant next attempt**.
3. The student can reopen the worksheet from the student portal. Their earlier submission remains in the attempt history.

Each grant is recorded in the staff audit log with the teacher, worksheet, student, and resulting number of approved extra attempts. **Reset attempts** deletes the student's attempt history and removes their grants; use it only when the history should be cleared.
